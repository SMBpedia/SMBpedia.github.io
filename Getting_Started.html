Getting Started

Emulator

The following emulators are allowed for the leaderboard:

  • Nestopia
  • NestopiaRTA
  • BizHawk (NesHawk core only)
  • OpenEmu (Nestopia core only)
  • MesenRTA
  • Mesen2RTA

NestopiaRTA and Mesen2RTA are the top two recommended emulators. NestopiaRTA tends to have better performance, especially on lower-end hardware, while Mesen2RTA has more features.

You also require a ROM of the game to play on emulator, which cannot be provided as it is copyrighted material. However, you can verify that you have a valid ROM by comparing the checksum to a list of checksums for known good ROMs.

Download Page

NestopiaRTA is a fork of Nestopia that adds features for timing and verifying runs.
