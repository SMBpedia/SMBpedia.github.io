8-4

8-4 is the final level of the game. Because timing ends immediately when Mario touches the axe, it is the only level where the time doesn't depend on framerules. Therefore, the most optimal strategy in 8-4 is to save as many frames as possible.

The path through the level involves going through five rooms.
